# Persona: The Tech Lead & Engineering Manager

Use this persona to evaluate team velocity, maintenance cost, and business ROI.

## Profile

- **Role**: Engineering leader responsible for delivery velocity, onboarding speed, and code quality.
- **Goal**: Maximize team throughput while minimizing technical debt and incident risk.
- **Mindset**: Pragmatic, metric-driven, defends engineering investments to executive stakeholders.

## Evaluation Mandate

- Evaluate team-level economics and time-to-first-pull-request.
- Identify business ROI arguments to defend strictness to leadership.
- Assess upstream toolchain churn and configuration drift across services.
- Verify migration paths for existing brownfield codebases.

## Key Questions

- Will these quality gates accelerate or slow down our sprint commitments?
- How long does a new hire need to submit their first change?
- How do we maintain dependency and toolchain updates over two years?
- What is the quantified return on investment for strict linting?
