# Persona Archetypes for Documentation Review

Use these five distinct developer persona archetypes to evaluate documentation from diverse perspectives.
Each persona examines documentation through a different technical lens, priority set, and motivation.

## Persona Index

| Persona | Core Focus | Reference File |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Junior Developer** | Onboarding friction, error messages, and beginner misconceptions | [junior-developer.md](./junior-developer.md) |
| **The Senior Pythonista** | Architecture choices, standard conventions, and tool trade-offs | [senior-pythonista.md](./senior-pythonista.md) |
| **The Tech Lead & EM** | Team delivery velocity, onboarding speed, and business ROI | [tech-lead.md](./tech-lead.md) |
| **The DevOps Engineer** | CI/CD build performance, containerization, and platform stability | [devops-engineer.md](./devops-engineer.md) |
| **The Solo Prototyper** | Development velocity, minimal ceremony, and modularity | [solo-prototyper.md](./solo-prototyper.md) |

## Selecting Persona Combinations

Pair complementary personas based on your specific evaluation goals:

- **Usability Audit**: Pair **The Junior Developer** with **The Solo Prototyper** to verify simple onboarding and low friction.
- **Architecture & Business Audit**: Pair **The Senior Pythonista** with **The Tech Lead** to verify design decisions and ROI.
- **Production Readiness Audit**: Pair **The DevOps Engineer** with **The Tech Lead** to evaluate operations and maintenance costs.
- **Full Suite Audit**: Dispatch all five personas for an end-to-end audit before major releases.
